Rules
RULEWHAT IT MEANS
A chat belongs to one strategyEvery chat has a strategy id. A strategy can have several chats (new day, new question, a long thread). An unsaved Untitled strategy owns its chats too, and keeps them when saved.
Opening a strategy opens its last chatThe panel opens that strategy’s most recent chat, scrolled to the end. If the strategy changed since, Copilot adds one live note at the end saying what changed. A strategy with no chats yet shows its empty state. New chat (+) starts a fresh one.
Copilot always knows the strategyEvery message carries the live blocks, Pine Script and backtest headline, plus the strategy notes. A new chat does not replay old transcripts.
Strategy notesCopilot writes short notes (intent, what was tried, what was rejected) when a chat goes quiet. Visible and editable in strategy ⋯ → What Copilot remembers.
Old cards are recordsIn an older chat, cards lose Undo / Apply and get a date chip (“Applied · 2 Nov”). The live strategy is the truth; Copilot points out what changed since.
History = chats, not versionsThe clock icon lists chats: this strategy first, then other strategies by name, then No strategy. There is no strategy version history.
Other strategies are read-onlyCopilot can read another saved strategy when asked (“compare with RSI Dip”) and only ever changes the open one. Opening another strategy’s chat shows it read-only.
Delete takes its chatsDeleting a strategy deletes its chats; the confirm says so. Duplicating copies the notes, not the chats.
